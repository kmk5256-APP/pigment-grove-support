Pigment Grove

Support for iPhone and iPad

Color and save a page

Choose a drawing in the Coloring Library. Use Fill to color a region with a tap, or choose Pencil or Marker to draw. On a compatible iPad, you can draw with Apple Pencil. Open Page Options to turn on Apple Pencil only.

Pigment Grove saves changes on your device. Open My Pages to pick up where you left off. To save a PNG or send a copy, tap the Share Artwork button on an open page.

Get help

Email support@ai2life.org with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened.

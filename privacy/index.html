Privacy Policy

Pigment Grove · November 19, 2026

Coloring data

Pigment Grove does not collect or transmit personal data through the app. The coloring library works offline. The app does not require an account and uses no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Your drawings, filled regions, favorites, and drawing preferences are saved on your device. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple. To remove local app data, delete Pigment Grove from your device.

Sharing artwork

When you share a page, Pigment Grove creates a PNG and opens the system share sheet. You choose where the copy goes. The destination you choose handles the copy according to its own privacy practices.

Support

If you email us, we use your message and email address to respond to your request. Reach us at support@ai2life.org.
